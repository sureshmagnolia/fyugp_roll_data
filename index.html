Nominal Roll Extractor

Upload your "NR_Third Semester..." PDF file, and this app will extract the schedule into a CSV format, complete with Room Numbers.

How to Use:

  1. Select your PDF Nominal Roll file (or a combined PDF of multiple rolls).
  2. Click 'Run Extraction'.
  3. The button will show a spinner. Wait for processing.
  4. A green download button will appear with your file (e.g., `Test1.csv`).
  5. The final CSV will include a "Room No" column, correctly sorted by date.

Disclaimer:

This tool uses automated logic to extract data. Due to variations in PDF formatting, the extracted data may contain errors. Please double-check the results.
